Project Overview

"AssetGuardian AI" — Industrial IoT Predictive Maintenance & Vibration Logs

The Problem: Heavy machinery like gas turbine compressors and industrial generators experience micro-vibrations that precede component wear. Maintenance teams miss early degradation signals when parsing siloed CSV logs or manual inspection schedules.

The Users: Reliability Engineers, Plant Operations Directors, and Field Service Technicians.

The Solution: An AI-augmented monitoring matrix that streams mock/live IoT sensor telemetry (frequency, harmonic variance, bearing temperature), flags out-of-spec thresholds, and generates an automated AI remediation playbook. concept prototype,

What This Is
  • An interactive concept prototype demonstrating real-time IoT anomaly detection.
  • A multi-node telemetry dashboard simulating industrial turbine and generator feeds.
  • A demonstration of automated AI remediation playbooks and 3D visual health mapping.
What This Is Not
  • Not connected to live production data (runs on simulated mock telemetry).
  • Not a fully functional production system (some auxiliary links or edge cases are out of scope).
  • Not a substitute for official SCADA hardware safety interlock controls.
